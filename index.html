Gainwork brings together everyone involved in the job placement process — eliminating the spreadsheets, phone tag, and disconnected tools that slow down real results.

Track your job search progress, receive personalized coaching, build your resume with AI, and stay connected with your job coach — all in one place.

About Gainwork

Built by People Who've Done This Work

Gainwork was created by Rich Alvarez — a workforce development professional with over a decade of experience placing hundreds of people into jobs, including justice-involved individuals navigating the path back to stable employment.

After years of managing caseloads with spreadsheets, sticky notes, and disconnected tools, Rich built Gainwork to solve the problems he lived every day. Every feature exists because a real job coach needed it.

Gainwork is a product of Alvarez & Associates LLC, a consulting firm specializing in workforce development, AI integration, and nonprofit operations.
