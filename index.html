How It Works

One Platform. Three Portals. Zero Gaps.

Gainwork brings together everyone involved in the job placement process — eliminating the spreadsheets, phone tag, and disconnected tools that slow down real results.

👤

Job Seeker Portal

Track your job search progress, receive personalized coaching, complete assignments, and stay connected with your job coach — all in one place.

  • Progress tracking dashboard
  • Direct messaging with coach
  • Document uploads & resume tools
  • Job application log
🧑‍💼

Job Coach Dashboard

Manage your entire caseload from one screen. Track placements, coordinate with employers, and document outcomes for grant reporting.

  • Full caseload management
  • Placement & outcome tracking
  • Grant-ready reporting
  • Employer relationship tools
🏢

Employer Portal

Post opportunities, review pre-screened candidates, and manage your hiring pipeline — with direct access to job coaches who know their candidates.

  • Job posting management
  • Candidate pipeline view
  • Direct coach communication
  • Hiring status tracking

About Gainwork

Built by People Who've Done This Work

Gainwork was created by Rich Alvarez — a workforce development professional with over a decade of experience placing hundreds of people into jobs, including justice-involved individuals navigating the path back to stable employment.

After years of managing caseloads with spreadsheets, sticky notes, and disconnected tools, Rich built Gainwork to solve the problems he lived every day. Every feature exists because a real job coach needed it.

Gainwork is a product of Alvarez & Associates LLC, a consulting firm specializing in workforce development, AI integration, and nonprofit operations.
